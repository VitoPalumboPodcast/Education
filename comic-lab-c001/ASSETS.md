# Illustrazioni originali

Create il 15 ottobre 2026 con lo strumento integrato ImageGen, una generazione per scena. Nessuna foto di persone reali usata come riferimento. Output PNG originali conservati nel progetto locale, copie JPEG ottimizzate a 1280 px per l’app.

## Prompt comune

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: landscape 16:9 background for an accessible language-learning comic app for 15–16 year olds. Create one original clean editorial comic illustration. Exactly two fictional adolescents aged 15–16, one on the left and one on the right, facing and conversing with each other. Waist-up or knee-up, characters occupy lower two thirds; upper third mostly blank pale wall or sky for dialogue bubbles that the app overlays. Simple uncluttered environment with only essential objects. Friendly age-appropriate contemporary clothes, natural proportions, clear facial expressions and gestures, not childish. Crisp dark teal outlines, flat soft colours, cream background, muted teal, terracotta, pale blue. Soft daylight. No text, lettering, speech bubbles, logos, watermarks, other people, collage or panels. Wide horizontal composition.
